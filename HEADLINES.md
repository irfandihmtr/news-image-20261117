## 1. LEAD — Kerch Bridge road arch collapses after Ukrainian strike
- What: Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge (linking Russia to occupied Crimea) for
  a second day in a row; the road bridge's main arch collapsed into the Kerch
  Strait, road traffic suspended, rail arch still standing. Russia hit Kyiv's
  North and South bridges over the Dnipro in return. Zelenskyy called it a
  seven-stage operation weeks in the making; the Kremlin vowed to rebuild.
